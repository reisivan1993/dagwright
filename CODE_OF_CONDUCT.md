# Code of Conduct

DAGwright is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or employer.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the substance of disagreements.
- Welcome newcomers and make technical decisions transparently.
- Respect privacy and obtain consent before sharing others' information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes occur.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, sustained disruption,
doxxing, retaliation, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional community are unacceptable
in project spaces and when representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through the hosting platform. Do
not open a public issue containing sensitive details. Maintainers must handle reports promptly,
confidentially, impartially, and without conflicts of interest. Responses may include a private
warning, moderation, temporary exclusion, or permanent exclusion, proportionate to the behavior.
Retaliation against a reporter or participant in an investigation is prohibited.

This policy applies to all project spaces and to public representation of DAGwright. Enforcement
decisions may be appealed to an uninvolved maintainer. As governance matures, the project will
publish a dedicated conduct contact and a more formal escalation process.
